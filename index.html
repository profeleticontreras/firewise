Defensible Space at a Glance

Zone 0: 0 to 5 ftGravel, stone, or pavers. Nothing flammable against the home.
Zone 1: 5 to 30 ftClear dead plants, leaves, and needles. Space plants apart.
Zone 2: 30 to 100 ftTrim grass to 4 inches. Space shrubs and trees.
